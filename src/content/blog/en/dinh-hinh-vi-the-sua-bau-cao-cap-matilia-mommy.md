In the FMCG and mother-and-baby nutrition market, brand building goes beyond introducing products — it is how a brand conveys empathy for its customers. For Matilia, a prenatal milk brand from France, the goal is not only to provide nutrition for expecting mothers, but to become a trusted companion throughout pregnancy.

To affirm its position as a premium prenatal milk line, Matilia Mommy set out to upgrade its visual identity and communications content on digital platforms, getting closer to the modern community of expecting mothers.

## Professional visuals — elevating the brand

For an imported brand, imagery plays a decisive role in building trust. G12 Media worked with Matilia Mommy to build a consistent, professional visual system that expresses the refinement of a premium French prenatal milk brand. Designs were standardised in colour, layout and photographic style to make a polished impression from the very first glance. Step by step, Matilia Mommy raised its brand recognition and built a sense of trust with mothers across social platforms.

## Turning nutrition science into approachable content

Pregnancy nutrition information tends to be highly technical and dry. Matilia Mommy's content strategy therefore focused on transforming nutritional knowledge into warm, relatable sharing that suits the sensitive mindset of expecting mothers. Articles were built around the journey of motherhood, blending scientific information with an emotional perspective — easy to understand, easy to empathise with, and always conveying that the brand walks alongside mothers through every stage of pregnancy.

## Optimising visual experience so mothers choose with ease

Alongside content, G12 Media produced clear, intuitive media that helps customers recognise flavour lines and understand the nutritional value of each product. The visual system was designed methodically so expecting mothers can quickly distinguish flavours and grasp the information they need. This improved the digital experience and helped customers make decisions more easily.

With a clearly directed content and visual strategy, Matilia Mommy steadily built a premium-yet-approachable brand image within the online community of expecting mothers. The brand's channels recorded improved engagement and stronger connections with target customers. More importantly, Matilia Mommy is now known not merely as an imported nutrition product, but as a brand that understands the journey of motherhood.

The partnership between Matilia Mommy and G12 Media shows that the right communications strategy can help a brand claim a clearer position in customers' minds.
