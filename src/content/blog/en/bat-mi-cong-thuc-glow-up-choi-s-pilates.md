In the health and fitness industry, brand image is not merely a matter of aesthetics — it is how a studio conveys its philosophy and core values to customers. For Choi's Pilates Hoang Mai, Pilates is not just physical training but the art of listening to and understanding the body, nurturing balance from within. Building a refined, modern and inspiring brand image on digital platforms therefore became an essential step in spreading these values to the community.

Partnering with Choi's Pilates, G12 Media rolled out a brand-image upgrade strategy — a "glow-up formula" that made the studio not only visually striking but also more deeply connected with its target customers.

## Professional visuals — the foundation of a modern brand

A studio with modern facilities deserves a visual system that matches its class. G12 Media focused on building professional visuals for Choi's Pilates through carefully produced photo and video sets. From lighting and composition to camera angles, every element was designed to highlight the elegant training space and modern Pilates equipment. These visuals are not only aesthetically strong; they help customers clearly feel what training at the studio is like. As the visuals levelled up, the brand became more professional and trustworthy in customers' eyes.

## Multi-channel presence — reaching the right customers

In the digital age, presence across social platforms is key to reaching customers effectively. G12 Media built a methodical content strategy for Choi's Pilates' fanpage and TikTok. Content ranged from real training experiences and coach-led sessions to inspiring everyday moments. Viewers see not just a Pilates studio, but a positive community where people care for their health together. Consistent, regular content kept the brand strongly present and created natural touchpoints with potential customers.

## Sharing expertise — turning knowledge into inspiring content

One of Choi's Pilates' differentiators is its dedicated, highly skilled coaching team. G12 Media turned this advantage into valuable community content: posts and videos sharing Pilates knowledge, posture improvement, body care, and members' positive transformation stories. Rather than merely promoting services, the content educates and inspires — helping viewers understand the benefits of Pilates and feel supported on their wellness journey. This is also how the brand builds lasting customer relationships.

Thanks to a unified visual and content strategy, Choi's Pilates steadily "glowed up" its brand image across digital platforms. Its fanpage and TikTok became effective media channels that clearly express the studio's philosophy and dedication. A more professional image increased brand credibility, while valuable content built deeper customer connections — attracting new members, lifting conversion rates and strengthening long-term loyalty.
